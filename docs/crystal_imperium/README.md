# Pokémon Crystal Imperium

The Johto and Kanto adventure of **Pokémon Heart & Soul** (a Gen 2 remake on the
pokeemerald-expansion GBA engine), rebuilt around the team building, quality of life and
competitive difficulty of **Pokémon Emerald Imperium**, with every Gen 1–9 Pokémon catchable.

> **Status: in development (0.8.1-experimental).** The full Heart & Soul story is in and
> playable. All the systems listed below work and are tested in an emulator. Every trainer
> uses the smart AI, every story boss from the rival to Red has an Imperium-style team with all
> of its Pokémon at the level cap, the TM list is Emerald Imperium's, and HMs work without being
> taught. Nobody has played the whole story through yet, and the postgame rematches still have
> Heart & Soul's teams (at the level cap too).

## What you get

From the first step of a new game:

- **Key items:** Rare Candy Box (unlimited Rare Candies up to the level cap), Infinite Repel (an
  on/off switch), Pokémon Box Link (the PC anywhere), Training Kit (change nature or ability
  any time), PokéVial (full heal outside battle, never runs out).
- **DexNav** (START menu):
  - It shows each area's Pokémon by icon and name, seen or not: the area's own, the day's
    Wild Variety Pokémon and three hidden ones. What you run into in the grass or on the water
    is on the list (with Heart & Soul's randomizer on, the randomized Pokémon are). Pick any
    of them: A searches, R registers.
  - SELECT lists **every** Gen 1–9 Pokémon (all 1,074: every evolution stage, regional
    forms, Legendaries), and you can search for any of them anywhere. LEFT/RIGHT jump 100.
  - Walk or run up to a Pokémon found by searching to battle it: no sneaking needed. It stays
    where it appeared, in caves and on water too. In Dark Cave, Flash lights the whole cave, as
    in Gold, Silver and Crystal, and DexNav works there.
  - R repeats a search for the registered Pokémon.
  - Hidden Pokémon are also detected while walking.
  - Search levels improve the chances of egg moves, Hidden Abilities and held items.
- **Wild Variety:** every area gets a daily set of other Gen 1–9 Pokémon (up to six in the
  grass and three Water-types on the water, already evolved if the area's level allows it),
  shown in DexNav. 30% of wild encounters (adjustable) are one of them. The sets change every
  day.
- **Easy Catch:** every Poké Ball catches (can be turned off).
- **Minimal Grinding Mode** (every Pokémon battles as if it had 31 IVs and 0 EVs), **level caps**,
  ×1.5 EXP, an **EASY / NORMAL / HARD** difficulty selection, **Auto Run**
  (START > OPTION > AUTORUN), and a **move relearner** in the party menu.
- **Emerald Imperium-style level caps:** 15 before the first Gym, then 24, 33, 42, 50, 58, 66,
  75 and **85 for the Elite Four**, 90 in Kanto, and no cap after the Kanto League (HARD caps: a
  few levels lower until the Elite Four). Other trainers follow the same curve.
- **Six Pokémon for every Gym Leader** and the big story bosses, and **28 route mini-bosses**:
  strong trainers on the routes with competitive teams at your level cap, as in Emerald
  Imperium.
- **The level cap is the level of every boss, and nothing is above it:** bosses are at your cap
  (NORMAL or HARD caps, on every difficulty), and no trainer's, wild, DexNav, legendary or gift
  Pokémon is above it.
- **HARD plays by Emerald Imperium's and Radical Red's rules:** the SET battle style (no "Will
  you switch Pokémon?" after a knockout) and no Bag in trainer battles, on top of +10% trainer
  levels and HARD level caps.
- **A Gen 5 look:** Black/White-style battles (healthboxes, text box, FIGHT / BAG / POKÉMON /
  RUN buttons, a move box with types, PP and effectiveness) and a Black/White-style summary
  screen (A on the skills page shows IVs and EVs).
- **Emerald Imperium's TMs:** 198 reusable TMs. TM01–TM172 are Imperium's list in its order
  (without its two custom moves), TM173–TM198 the Heart & Soul TMs Imperium doesn't have. The
  Goldenrod Dept. Store 5F sells all of them, more with each badge tier (3, 6 and 8 badges).
- **HMs without teaching:** carrying the HM (and its badge) lets any Pokémon in the party use
  Cut, Surf, Strength, Rock Smash, Waterfall, and Fly or Flash from the lead's party menu.
- **Care packages** from Prof. Elm after every badge and after becoming Champion, full of
  battle gear the Emerald Imperium way: competitive held items (Leftovers, Life Orb, Focus Sash,
  Choice items, Assault Vest, Heavy-Duty Boots, Booster Energy and dozens more, the staples
  several times), every type-boosting item, every type-resist and pinch Berry, 93 PP Max over the
  game, X items and Elixirs, besides supplies and every evolution item the game doesn't
  otherwise have. Each package lists what's inside; if a Bag pocket is full, the rest goes to
  your PC.
- **Mega Evolution** (48 classic Megas plus Rayquaza; stones from Goldenrod Dept. Store 5F).
- Legendary and Mythical Pokémon are on the DexNav list from the start. Once you are
  Champion, about a quarter of each day's Wild Variety sets include one.
- **Cheat menu:** hold R and press START in the overworld for HnS's debug menu (give any
  Pokémon or item, and more; *Flags & Vars… → Pokédex Flags All* fills the Pokédex). Use it
  with care: it can also change story flags.
- **Smart AI for every trainer** on NORMAL and HARD: it knows your team, picks the best move,
  switches smartly and plays around HP (EASY keeps Heart & Soul's AI). Sleep Clause.
- **Imperium-style bosses for the whole story:** every Johto Gym Leader (Chuck, Jasmine and
  Pryce in three versions, for whichever order you take them), the rival from Azalea to
  Victory Road, Team Rocket's executives, the Kimono Girls, the Elite Four and Lance, the eight
  Kanto leaders, Blue and Red. **Every boss Pokémon is at your level cap** (not just the ace;
  with HARD level caps, the HARD cap), with held items, natures, abilities, competitive moves,
  and Mega Evolutions from the 5th Gym on. A boss you fight later than usual (the Rocket Hideout
  with six badges, a rematch after the Kanto League) is raised to your cap then; rematches after
  the Kanto League are at 100.
- **Red is the hardest battle in the game:** all six at level 100, competitive EVs that only his
  Pokémon keep under Minimal Grinding, the boss AI with move and switch prediction, four Full
  Restores, and a sun team around Mega Charizard Y. He battles only the Kanto Champion, so win
  the Kanto League first (the level cap is gone by then).

All of these are in the challenge menu at the start of a new game (and on the settings PC in
your house), except Auto Run, which is in the Options menu.

## Building

You do **not** need a Pokémon ROM; the game is compiled entirely from source.

On Ubuntu 24.04 / Debian (or WSL2 on Windows):

```sh
sudo apt install build-essential git libpng-dev gcc-arm-none-eabi binutils-arm-none-eabi libnewlib-arm-none-eabi
# optional, for the emulator play tests:
sudo apt install libmgba-dev
git clone https://github.com/Cvega6950/glowing-octo-potato.git
cd glowing-octo-potato
git checkout claude/pokemon-crystal-imperium-vsmmzc
tools/crystal_imperium/build.sh          # -> CrystalImperium.gba
tools/crystal_imperium/build.sh --test   # also run the emulator play tests
```

The first build takes a few minutes. Tested tool chain: arm-none-eabi-gcc 13.2.1, libmgba 0.10.2.

## Patches

`tools/crystal_imperium/make_patches.sh` writes both patches into `build/release/`:

- **Source patch series** (`git format-patch` on top of Heart & Soul's tag `2.0.6`): apply it
  to an HnS 2.0.6 checkout with `git am` and build as above.
- **BPS patch** from the Heart & Soul 2.0.6 ROM, built from that tag with the same tool chain,
  to `CrystalImperium.gba`. Apply it with Flips or Rom Patcher JS. `SHA1SUMS` lists both ROMs'
  hashes; the patch only applies to that exact HnS ROM. The script checks that the patch
  rebuilds the ROM byte for byte.

No patch against an original Nintendo ROM is provided, and no ROM files are distributed from
this repository.

## Playing on Delta (iPhone)

1. Get `CrystalImperium.gba` onto the phone. Either:
   - build it as above and copy it over (AirDrop, iCloud Drive or Files), or
   - make this GitHub repository **private**, then open *Actions → Crystal Imperium → latest
     run → Artifacts* and download `CrystalImperium-<commit>` on the phone. CI only uploads
     the ROM for private repositories, because the ROM contains Nintendo's copyrighted
     assets.
2. In Delta, tap **+** → **Files** and pick `CrystalImperium.gba`.
3. Use the in-game SAVE (START → SAVE). Delta keeps the cartridge save between sessions.
4. If the day/night clock misbehaves, open the settings PC in your house and set Clock Type
   to the ROM's fake clock.

Device testing on Delta has not been done yet. Please report what happens using the checklist
in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md).

## Saves

Saves from Heart & Soul 2.0.6 and from earlier Crystal Imperium builds load. On Continue they
receive any key items they are missing and the DexNav, and their TMs keep their moves under the
new numbering (Heart & Soul's TM70 Rock Climb becomes TM192, for example). Easy Catch and Wild Variety (30%) are on
for them, and any care packages for badges already earned arrive one per step. Minimal
Grinding starts OFF for HnS saves and can be turned on from the settings PC.
